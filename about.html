Our Story

All Type of EDM Work, Done Right

Sharp Axis was started with a simple goal — give tool rooms, mould makers and manufacturers in Rajkot a dependable partner for EDM (Electrical Discharge Machining) job work. Instead of juggling multiple vendors for sinker EDM, spark erosion and general job work, customers can bring every type of EDM job to one workshop.

Every component that comes in — whether it's a die plate, a mould core, a punch set or a broken tap that needs removing — is machined with the same attention to tolerance, finish and deadline.
