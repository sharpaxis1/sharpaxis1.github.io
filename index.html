What We Do

Our EDM Services

From sinker EDM cavity work to spark erosion and general job work, we handle all type of EDM work under one roof.

Sinker / Die-Sinking EDM

Cavity and core sinking for moulds, dies and forging tools using custom-shaped electrodes.

Spark Erosion Machining

Precision spark erosion work on hardened steels and alloys that are difficult to machine conventionally.

General Spark/EDM Job Work

Miscellaneous spark-erosion and EDM job work that doesn't fit neatly into a fixed category — quoted and handled case by case.

Tool, Punch & Die Repair

Repair and rework of worn punches, dies and tooling to restore accuracy and extend service life.
